Unlock the console

When pnpm agents starts, it prints a console link with a one-time token. Open that link, or paste the token here.

running 2 agent(s) on local: foo, tap console: http://127.0.0.1:7070/#token=[token]

Requests without this token, or from a page not served at 127.0.0.1:7070, are refused. That stops other websites open in your browser from steering Rock or spending money. A new token is issued each time the host starts.
